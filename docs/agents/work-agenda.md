# Work: agenda and calendar

A linked Work workspace's Agenda view, the held calendar act's own view in the Outbox, and plain holds as Results (milestone 25). LifeOS's `calendarOps` are milestone 24's (`landed/M24.md`).

## The view

`src/renderer/src/work/agenda/AgendaView.tsx`, a lazy chunk from `agenda/view.ts`: List, the sector's next 14 days by day through the shared agenda client (`useEvents(from, to)` on `events:range` with the sector), each event at its time with where it is and when to leave; Week and Month are LifeOS's own (work-kit's `AgendaWeek` and `AgendaMonth`), and an event or a day picked there opens that day's list. Where events at once leave a Week block under 64px wide, `agenda/agenda.css` keeps its title on one line cut whole and drops its time line (a container query on work-kit's block; `agendaWeek.dom.test.tsx` fails if a work-kit pin draws the block in other shapes). A Month day's number, 20px tall and as wide as its digits, takes a press 24px each way from the same file (an `::before` over the cell's padding). A List row's title runs the row's width at rest; its hover verb is drawn over the title's end on the row's own fill (the page under the row's hover tint), its first 12px fading the title out into that tint, as a rail row's actions do, its 24px press centred on the title's line; a title or place the column cuts is said whole in the row's one tip, on hover and focus, only while one is cut (`useTruncated`, as a Today row's); its time column is 88px, as wide as "Leave 12:45 PM" on one line. A press anywhere on the row opens the meeting's page (an `ItemButton` laid over the row, named "Prepare Budget review" or "Follow up Staff sync"); the verb is its pointer's shortcut and takes no Tab stop. The clash lines and the attendee names keep their own presses above it, each name's hit area 24px on its 16px line. ↑ and ↓ move between the events across the days (`moveBetweenRows`, work/rowKeys.ts). The header gives way as it narrows: under 40rem the full period becomes the short one ("Nov 23 – 29", the en dash work-kit's period uses too) and List, Week and Month become one select; Today is offered only off today's week or month (`holdsToday`), where it has somewhere to go; under 26rem its word goes (and every Work view's header drops its glyph, WorkViewPage) and a 28px icon press with a Today tip stands in its place, so the way back is one press; Previous and Next stay, each with its tip, under 40rem New event's words give way to a 28px icon press with its tip, the short period stays whole, and in a Week or a Month the view's name gives way whole (Agenda's own `Title`, still read aloud and still the heading's focus), never cut to "Age…", since the column names the view. While events load, the bones are the view he kept: the list's rows on the rows' own grid and height (a day's heading, then a time beside a title over a shorter place line, 36px a row), or the Week's or the Month's grid. An invitation's Open in Agenda in Mail opens the view with the event (`openWorkView({…, view: 'agenda', event})`, taken once): Agenda reads it (`events:get`) and opens Week at its day, whatever month it is in; until the read answers it draws its bones, so today's list never paints first. A row's verb opens the meeting's page ([work-prep.md](work-prep.md), `openWorkView({…, view: 'prep', event})`): it reads Prepare until the event ends and Follow up after, on the row's hover and in its right-click, and opening the page starts no thread. A new thread about the event (`{kind: 'event', …, ref: {at: 'lifeos', table: 'events', id}}`) is in that page's More. The column's Agenda row shows today's next event's time (`useNextEvent`, tasks/facts.ts).

New event (`agenda/NewEventDialog.tsx`) is a calendar act LifeOS holds (`calendarOps:save {kind: 'create', sourceId}` on one of the sector's Google calendars), sent by his press in the dialog, Add or Send invites (`calendarOps:send`). Once the sources are read, a job with no Google calendar that takes events says so where a session's line stands ("Purdue Esports has no calendar that takes new events.") with Cancel alone: no fields and no Add, and what he typed stays in the form's state for a calendar that comes; a refusal with no words of its own reads "LifeOS did not add the event". Its dialog is the frame's (`Kept`): a session that drops while it is open keeps what he typed and the calendars it read, says `DownLine` in it and holds Add until the session reads again. Day, From and To share a row where the dialog is 420px or wider inside (the room the day in words, "Thu, Nov 26, 2026", needs beside two times) (a container query, `@container/when`); narrower, Day takes the row and From and To share the next, 16px under it as every field is under the one before. It has no thread origin, so it never lists in the Outbox. His press only queues the change in LifeOS, so the toast claims nothing early (`agenda/added.ts` `tellAdded`): it reads "Adding …" ("Sending invites: …" with attendees) and watches LifeOS's own row (`calendarOps:get`) to `done` ("Added …", "Invites sent: …") or `failed` ("… was not added" with Google's reason); with no answer in 45 s it says the event is queued in LifeOS.

A List row names the event's attendees who are the sector's people ("With Maya Chen, Tom Beck, and 2 more"), each name opening their page in People ([work-people.md](work-people.md)); the people come from `people:directory` on the view's session.

A row whose event clashes with another, in any job, says so under its place and offers Show clash in its menu, until the clash's later event is over; its Leave is the clash rule's once that has answered ([work-clashes.md](work-clashes.md): `useClashes(env, from, to)` in `work/clashes/mark.tsx`, read on the owner session, since the view's own session reads one job). Until it has, the row keeps LifeOS's own leave-by, so nothing is blank while the read is out.

## `calendar.op` in the Outbox

The kind is held and sent by the hold (`work/outbox/lifeosHeld.ts`): the watcher reads `calendarOps:held {host}`, and the words come from the act's kind, payload and the event's title (read once per event with `events:get`). At the press the act is read again (`calendarOps:get`): a draft or failed act goes with `calendarOps:send {id, cause}`; one edited since it was shown goes stale; one already queued, sending or done is not sent twice. Edit is `calendarOps:save` with the act's own kind and event (and, for a new Google event, the source of the same account's calendar). A grant (`CalendarOpMatcherSchema`) fits one kind on one account's one calendar: an invitation's answer, or an invite that names its people. A change, a cancel, a proposal, a series or an uninvite is always his press.

Its view (`agenda/CalendarActView.tsx`, chunk `work-calendar-act`) shows every field that goes (`opFields` in `agenda/calendarAct.ts`; a server test holds each `CalendarFieldsSchema` key to a row), then that day's calendar, every sector's, read as the owner, with the act's slot set apart: its bones while LifeOS reads it, and the day from local midnight to the next (`addDays`, as Agenda's own days), so it is 23 or 25 hours on a day the clocks change. Invites names each guest as Mail's cards do: the name, then the address muted after it. Calendar names an account's own calendar by its address and a shared one by the name of the source LifeOS syncs it through (`sources:list`, `calendarWords`), the account after it only where two synced calendars share that name; a provider's id is never shown (not synced, the account alone). While that list is read, a calendar named by its id has a bone in its row, never the address a moment later replaced by the name. A reply or a change whose act leaves out its event's title or time waits on `events:get`: bones in those rows while it reads, then "Couldn't read the event" with Retry when the read failed, or "The event is no longer in LifeOS" when it found nothing. A Retry there, and the day's, asks a live session again; when the LifeOS session itself is down, one line in its place says the session's state in the shared words with the one press that can help (`shell/lifeosDown.ts`: Retry for "LifeOS did not answer", which asks the host for the session again; Sign in for a sign-in missing or refused and Show the code for one that waits, where this window may sign the host in; Open Settings for Work off where it may turn Work on; else who can; none for a computer the host keeps LifeOS from). Display only: the hold is untouched.

## Plain holds

A create on his own calendar that invites no one runs at once in LifeOS (`plain: true`) and is never held. `agenda/index.ts` watches `calendarOps:list {limit: 50}` and records each one this host's live threads made as a Result of its thread, once it is `done`, once (`work_agenda_results`), and only for acts made since the host first watched.
