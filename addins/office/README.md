# TempCode's Office add-in

A task pane add-in for Excel, Word and PowerPoint. Its pages are served by the
Work service at `/w/addin/*`; the manifest names
`https://tempcode.landonhale.com/w/addin/taskpane.html`. How it connects, how it
pairs, and how to sideload it on the Mac and in Excel for the web:
`docs/agents/work-office.md`, "The add-in".
